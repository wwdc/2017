# WWDC 2017 - Scholarship

![WWDC 2017 Logo](logo.png)

List of student submissions for the WWDC 2017 - Scholarship. The list is in alphabetical order.

### How to add your submission?
1. **[Click here to open the form](https://github.com/wwdc/2017/issues/new?template=submit.yml)** and fill it in.
2. Send it. A bot checks your entry within a minute and tells you if anything needs fixing. You can edit the form after sending.
3. When it is approved your entry appears on this list.

#### How to update or remove your submission?
Use the **[update form](https://github.com/wwdc/2017/issues/new?template=update.yml)**. Only the person who sent the entry can change it.
